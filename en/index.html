Research Interest: Action-Conditioned Generative Video World Models

Select only papers whose central contribution is a learned visual world model that generates future video frames from visual history and agent/environment actions:

p(x_{t+1:t+H} | x_{<=t}, a_{t:t+H})

The output must be viewable future frames/video (possibly decoded from video tokens or latents), and the action must causally control the generated evolution at inference time.

Required for Selection

A paper must satisfy all of the following:

  1. It introduces or materially improves a generative visual dynamics/world model.
  2. It conditions future-frame or future-video generation on explicit actions (e.g., keyboard/controller inputs, robot controls, ego-vehicle controls, or language-specified environment actions), or on learned latent actions designed for frame-by-frame interaction.
  3. It supports sequential action-conditioned rollout, interactive simulation, or closed-loop prediction—not merely one-shot prompt-to-video generation.
  4. Its main technical contribution addresses action controllability, dynamics/physics fidelity, temporal or long-horizon consistency, autoregressive stability, generalization, or real-time generation.

Relevant domains include games, robotics, autonomous driving, and open-domain interactive environments. Diffusion, autoregressive, and token/latent-based approaches are all eligible if they ultimately generate visual observations.

Strict Exclusions

Do not select:

  • Generic text-to-video or image-to-video generation without step-wise action control.
  • Passive video prediction conditioned only on past frames, text, initial conditions, or camera/object trajectories that are not agent/environment actions.
  • Video editing, camera control, animation, human-motion generation, or “action-conditioned” generation where action only means an action class such as running or dancing.
  • Action recognition, action forecasting, inverse dynamics, or models that predict actions from video rather than video from actions.
  • Conventional latent world models that predict only hidden states, rewards, values, or policies and do not generate viewable future observations.
  • Generic model-based RL, planning, control, robot-policy/VLA, or autonomous-driving papers where video generation is not the core contribution.
  • 3D/4D reconstruction, novel-view synthesis, neural rendering, or physics simulators without a learned action-conditioned video generator.
  • Surveys, benchmarks, datasets, evaluations, and system/demo papers without a substantial new modeling or training method.

Scoring Rule

Be precision-first. A paper should receive RELEVANCE=true and RELEVANCE_SCORE >= 8 only when action-conditioned visual future generation is the paper's primary task and all required conditions are clear from the title or abstract. Give adjacent or ambiguous papers at most 6 and set RELEVANCE=false. Prefer missing borderline work over collecting loosely related world-model or video-generation papers.
